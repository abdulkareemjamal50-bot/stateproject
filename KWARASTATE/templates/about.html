Who We Are

Discover the Heart of KWARA STATE

Kwara State is one of Nigeria's most peaceful and culturally diverse states, located in the North Central region of the country. Known as the "State of Harmony," it is home to people from different ethnic groups who live together in unity and mutual respect. Created on May 27, 1967, Kwara State has grown into a center for agriculture, education, commerce, tourism, and cultural heritage. Its capital, Ilorin, is renowned for its rich history, hospitality, and blend of traditional and modern lifestyles. Our mission is to showcase the beauty, history, opportunities, and achievements of Kwara State while providing residents, visitors, investors, and tourists with reliable and up-to-date information

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To deliver transparent, inclusive, and people-centered governance by providing quality public services, promoting sustainable development, and improving the standard of living for all residents of Kwara State.

Our Vision

To build a prosperous, peaceful, and innovative Kwara State where every citizen has equal access to opportunities, quality infrastructure, excellent education, healthcare, and sustainable economic growth.

Our Core Values

Integrity, accountability, transparency, excellence, innovation, inclusiveness, respect for the rule of law, and dedicated service to the people of Kwara State.
